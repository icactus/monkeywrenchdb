# Preparing the standalone Suite No. 1 tutorial

`bachsuite1-tutorial` is a reserved preparation piece, separate from Bach’s full
Cello Suite No. 1 (piece 27). The original Gigue upload is queue entry
`6244fa3f7bcc4fbfa351861d8330067c`, named `bachsuite1-tutorial.pdf` and assigned
to Cello (instrument 4). The original uploaded bytes remain unchanged.

Open **Edit barlines** in the PDF queue, edit the page, and submit it. This saves
a normal metric row and PDF so the preparation piece is loadable in YubSync.
Its queue metadata carries `private_tutorial: true`; draft and publication
requests cannot move it into a public piece. The published PDF follows the
normal numeric piece/part filename convention. The queue name and piece label
are `bachsuite1-tutorial`.

In YubSync, choose `bachsuite1-tutorial`, load Cello, paste the standalone
movement’s YouTube URL, and submit the manually recorded sync normally. There
is no recording for this piece until the administrator submits one.

The reserved piece is excluded from both catalog readers, public snapshot ID
maps, public piece landing pages, and the sitemap, including after barlines or
recordings are saved. Admin dropdowns continue to include it. This controls
library visibility; the normal PDF and static data paths are still used.

After the administrator finishes the standalone recording, rebuild the frozen
practice exercise from this new metric/recording pair. Do not use the full-suite
recording’s excerpt as the final reference. Publishing the preparation workflow
does not replace the current practice exercise or its official timestamps.
