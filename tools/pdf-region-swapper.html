How pages are kept tidy

Pages keep their original dimensions. A passage that fits replaces the cut area directly. Larger passages use available space, then continue on a new page of the same size if needed. Music is never cut in half to make it fit.

If page widths differ, a wider passage is reduced to fit. You will see the result before downloading.
